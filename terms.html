What the app does

myFloatLyric shows the lyrics of the song playing in another music app, in a floating window over your screen. It does not play music itself. Lyrics come from third-party lyrics services, from a shared cache filled by other users, or — when nothing else is found — from an on-device AI transcription of the song.

Lyrics and copyright

Lyrics belong to their songwriters and publishers. The app displays them for your personal, non-commercial use while you listen. Accuracy

Lyrics and their timing come from third parties and other users and may be incomplete, wrong or out of sync with your version of the song. AI transcription is experimental and often makes mistakes, especially with singing over music. The app does not guarantee that the lyrics shown are correct.

Contributing lyrics

With “Contribute lyrics to the cloud” turned on, lyrics the app finds, and timings it works out, are added to a shared cache that other users of the app can read. You can switch this off in Settings at any time.
