Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

myFloatLyric has no accounts. Almost everything the app keeps is stored only on your own phone, and you can delete it yourself. The one exception is lyrics contributed to the shared cache, which carry no information about who contributed them — see below.

Delete data on your phone

  • Lyrics cache: open the app → Settings (设置) → Cache (缓存管理) → “Clear lyrics cache” (清空歌词缓存). “Clear not-found records” (清除未找到记录) removes the list of songs recently not found.
  • Speech model (AI transcription): Settings → AI transcription (AI 听写) → “Delete” (删除) next to the model.
  • Everything: go to your phone's Settings → Apps → 悬浮歌词 → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes the cache, the speech model and all your settings.

Audio used for AI transcription is never saved, so there is nothing to delete.

Lyrics in the shared cache

If “Contribute lyrics to the cloud” (贡献歌词到云端) was on, lyrics the app found were added to the shared cache with the song's title, artist, album and length. These entries contain no account, device identifier or personal information, so they cannot be traced back to you or deleted “per user”.

  • To stop contributing, turn the setting off in the app's Settings.
  • To have specific lyrics removed from the shared cache, email us the song title and artist and we will delete them.

Third parties

  • Advertising: the app uses Google AdMob. Data tied to your advertising identifier is handled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your phone's settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads). See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Lyrics services: requests sent to lyrics services contain only song information, not personal data. Any logs they keep are governed by their own policies.
