Synced lyrics, floating over any music app

myFloatLyric (悬浮歌词) watches what's playing and floats the lyrics on top of your screen, scrolling line by line with the song.

🎧

Works with your player

YouTube Music, Spotify, your phone's built-in player — any app that shows playback controls. You choose which ones pop up lyrics.

🎤

Line-by-line sync

The current line is highlighted as the song plays, from several lyrics sources and a shared cache that gets better as people listen.

🪟

Stays out of the way

Drag it anywhere, lock it so taps go straight through to the app underneath, and it hides by itself when the music stops.

✨

On-device AI transcription

No lyrics found? An experimental offline speech model can transcribe the song as it plays. The audio never leaves your phone.
